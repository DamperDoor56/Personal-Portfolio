I'm Ludmila, passionate dev with almost 4 years of professional experience and trough these years, I've been working on freelance, startups, and software factories working in both Back-End, Front-End, and DevOps area, managing cloud infrastructure, particularly Azure and AWS, and a bit of Google Cloud.
